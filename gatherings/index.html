A Retreat, Not a To-Do List

Ground & Gaze is intentionally different from a traditional retreat, workshop, or business getaway. There is no expectation that you attend every workshop, participate in every activity, or fill every hour. The itinerary offers structure and possibility; your body gets to vote.

Listen to what you need. Follow your intuition. Move when you feel called to move. Rest when you need rest. Spend time in conversation, or enjoy your own company.

Come to the experiences that nourish you. Take a nap. Soak in the spa. Journal. Walk the land. Sit beneath the stars. Explore Joshua Tree. Or simply do nothing at all.

You don't need to accomplish anything here.
You simply get to arrive, exhale, and listen.

Ground & Gaze

Ground & Gaze is an intimate women's wellness retreat beneath the Taurids, taking place in Joshua Tree National Park from November 6–8, 2026.

This retreat is an invitation to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and experience the vastness of the desert sky in the company of a small circle of women.

The weekend will include yoga and Qigong, emotional renewal, nourishment, Reiki, sound baths, and meteor shower stargazing.

The retreat is intentionally different from a traditional retreat, workshop, or business getaway. There is no expectation that you attend every workshop, participate in every activity, or fill every hour.

Listen to what you need. Follow your intuition. Move when you feel called to move. Rest when you need rest. Spend time in conversation, or enjoy your own company.

Come to the experiences that nourish you. Take a nap. Soak in the spa. Journal. Walk the land. Sit beneath the stars. Explore Joshua Tree. Or simply do nothing at all.

Your retreat includes two nights in a luxury private desert villa with shared accommodations, farm-to-table meals and nourishing refreshments, guided yoga and Qigong, somatic wellness practices, Earth & Sky guided meditation, Yin Yoga, sound baths, a Reiki experience, and a desert Taurid stargazing and ritual setting experience.

The gathering is limited to 15 women, creating an intimate space for connection, reflection, and renewal.
